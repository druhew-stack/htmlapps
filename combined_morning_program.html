Combined Morning Protocol — 4 Weeks

Tendon and balance,
woven together.

A single morning routine merging Achilles loading with the SLSEC progression. Both systems train through the same four-week arc — foundation, progression, loading with instability, integration and test. About 15–20 minutes daily.

Five Principles for Both Systems

The work matters more than the numbers.

— 01
Tempo over reps
Slow eccentrics drive tendon adaptation. Bouncy reps don't. Three seconds down is non-negotiable.
— 02
Load progresses, not reps
Once 3×12 slow feels comfortable, add weight before adding reps. Tendons want load, not endless volume.
— 03
Balance is a skill
It's not a strength outcome — it's neural. Daily small doses build it. Wobbling is the work; missing days isn't.
— 04
Discomfort fine, sharp pain isn't
A low-grade ache during loading is normal tendon work. Sharp pain or pain lingering past 24 hours means back off.
— 05
Fuel the tendon
Take ~15g gelatin or hydrolysed collagen with vitamin C 30–60 min before this routine. Stack the signals.

Run this past whoever prescribed your original calf-raise protocol before starting. Adjust loads and balance progressions to your tolerance.
