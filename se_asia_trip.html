Fri · 10 Jul

Gardens by the Bay + Night Safari

Today's logic

Keep the daytime half light — Gardens in the morning only, then a proper rest before heading out again at night. Night Safari runs 7pm to 10:30pm, so build in a nap window mid-afternoon.

  • BrekkyToast Box or Ya Kun — kaya toast, soft-boiled eggs, kopi. The kids' intro to local breakfast.
  • MorningGardens by the Bay — Cloud Forest + Flower Dome combo. Book online ahead. Indoor, air-conditioned, keep it to ~2 hours.
  • LunchSatay by the Bay — outdoor hawker stalls right at Gardens.
  • AfternoonBack to the hotel — pool, nap, quiet time. Banking energy for tonight.
  • Pre-dinnerEat at Night Safari before the show — Ulu Ulu Safari Restaurant or Casa Italia. Don't try to dinner in town first or you'll be racing the clock.
  • 19:00 — 22:30Night Safari. Catch the 7:30pm Creatures of the Night show, do the tram ride (~40 min), then walk Fishing Cat Trail. Book tickets online for ~10% discount, direct entry, no queue.

Sat · 11 Jul

Bay East Garden parkrun + Sentosa

Early start today

Bay East Garden is the closest parkrun to Boat Quay — flat, paved, 5k, two laps. Since you're all already registered from Sydney parkrun, just bring your usual barcodes.

  • 07:00Grab to Marina Bay East Garden (11 Rhu Cross) — meet under Sheares Bridge, next to the Passion Wave carpark. ~15 min from Boat Quay.
  • 07:30parkrun start — flat, paved 5k out-and-back along the Bay East skyline, two laps. No time limit, kids under 11 stay within arm's reach of an adult. Toilets and a drinks machine at the start/finish.
  • ~08:30 – 09:00Coffee and a quick breakfast at Kontiki with the other parkrunners — the usual post-run routine.
  • 09:00Grab back to the hotel to shower and change, then on to Sentosa.
  • ~10:00Grab (XL) straight to Universal Studios, not MRT — one-hop door-to-door via Sentosa Gateway, ~15–20 min. Worth the ~S$25–35 given the later, already-busy morning; MRT + Sentosa Express means two transfers and more walking with the boys.
  • ActivityUniversal Studios — the strong pick for 7/10/12 boys. The 7yo clears the 122 cm threshold for nearly everything; the older two will love Battlestar Galactica and Transformers.
  • LunchMalaysian Food Street on Sentosa — hawker-style, indoors, AC.
  • DinnerFamily dinner — Saturday night with family. Suggestions if choosing the spot: Jumbo Seafood at Riverside Point for chilli crab with a view, or Putien for round-table Fujian.

Thu · 16 Jul

ESCAPE water park

The must-do

ESCAPE Theme Park in Teluk Bahang — 15 min from Batu Ferringhi by Grab. World's longest water slide (1,140m), wave pool, lazy river, plus a dry adventure park if anyone needs a break from the water. Plan a full day.

  • BrekkyHotel — fuel up properly, it's a big day.
  • 10:00 — 16:00ESCAPE Theme Park. Buy the Waterplay + Adventureplay combo. Slather sunscreen. Take the Gravityplay drop slides for the older two, lazy river for everyone. The world's longest water slide (Penang Bucket List Slide) is the centrepiece — about 4 minutes top to bottom.
  • LunchAt ESCAPE — there are food stalls inside (basic but fine). Or stop on the way back at Khaleel Restaurant in Batu Ferringhi for a nasi kandar reset.
  • AfternoonBack to the resort. Pool optional — the boys may have had enough water for one day.
  • DinnerLast Penang dinner — Hameediyah (oldest nasi kandar in Penang, worth the taxi) or resort sunset cocktails.
