— PRIORITY FOCUS

Achilles Strengthening Days

The collagen + vitamin C window is the highest-leverage piece of this routine. Time it well.

If exercising in the morning

  1. Wake up: water + collagen 15 g + multivitamin together
  2. Wait 30–60 min (this is the synthesis window)
  3. Achilles exercises — short session, 5–10 minutes
  4. Smoothie + fish oil after the session (fats help the multi absorb)

If exercising later in the day

  1. Smoothie + multi + fish oil at usual breakfast time
  2. Wait at least 60–90 min for the meal to digest
  3. Collagen 15 g + small vitamin C source (OJ, kiwi)
  4. 30–60 min later, do your Achilles exercises
Why this matters Collagen creates a sharp amino acid spike that arrives at your tendon during loading. Vitamin C is a required cofactor for collagen synthesis. Together they amplify tendon remodelling — but only if the collagen is taken on a relatively empty stomach (no whey or big meal blunting the signal).

Guiding principles

  • Collagen pre-workout works because the amino acid signal arrives during loading. Don't blend it into the smoothie — keep the signal clean.
  • Vitamin C is required for collagen synthesis. Always pair them.
  • Multivitamin prefers food fats nearby (within an hour or so). The smoothie covers this.
  • Magnesium glycinate at dinner leverages the glycine for evening recovery.
  • Tendons respond to short, frequent loading — not long sessions. 5–10 minutes is plenty.
  • Race-day cramping is mostly about sodium and pacing, not magnesium.
  • Sleep, training, and food still matter more than any of these supplements.
A note This is general guidance based on what you've shared, not medical advice. Worth getting a baseline blood panel (vitamin D, B12, ferritin, lipids, HbA1c) at some point so you can check what's actually working. Run any meaningful changes past your GP — especially if you're on medications or have existing conditions.
