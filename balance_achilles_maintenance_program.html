The rules that don't change.
These carry across from your original program. The physio's update changed the loading-day exercises, not these principles.
01
Left leg first, always
The lagging side leads — on the calf raises, the soleus raises, the glute bridges, and the balance work. It gets the extra balance set too.
02
The 24-hour stiffness check is the gate
Judge each session by how the tendon feels the next morning. Stiffer than usual = too much; ease the dose. This gate decides any change, not the calendar.
03
Reps over load, per physio
Lighter weight, build to 20 controlled reps. Don't chase the old heavy loads. If 20 feels genuinely easy on both legs, check with her before adding weight.
04
Warm up before loading, not before SLEC
Loading days always start with the tendon warm-up — especially Monday after the rest day. SLEC days need no heavy warm-up.
05
Collagen on loading days only
~15g hydrolysed collagen + vitamin C, 30–60 min before the loading session. Not before SLEC, not on pure cycling days.
06
Discomfort fine, sharp pain isn't
A low-grade ache during loading is normal. Sharp pain, or pain lingering past 24 hours, means ease off and check in with her.
