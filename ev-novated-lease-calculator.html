Used EV — confirm FBT eligibility before relying on these numbers. A second-hand EV qualifies for the FBT exemption only if it was first held and used after 1 July 2022 AND luxury car tax was never payable on it (i.e. its original retail price was below the LCT threshold of the year it was first sold). Most used Teslas, BYDs, MG4s, EV6s, Atto 3s and similar mainstream EVs qualify; used premium imports (Model S/X, BMW iX, Mercedes EQE/EQS, Audi e-tron) generally do not.

Caveats & assumptions

Tax brackets 2025-26

Uses ATO resident brackets plus 2% Medicare levy. Stage 3 cuts already applied. Doesn't model HECS/HELP — note that the FBT-exempt amount still appears as a Reportable Fringe Benefit on your income statement and can lift adjusted taxable income for HELP repayment thresholds.

GST credit cap

Capped at the GST on the ATO car cost limit ($69,674 for 2025-26 → max $6,334 of GST credit). Vehicles above this only get partial GST relief. Available on used EVs too if purchased through a registered dealer.

Residual value

Set to ATO statutory minimums: 65.63% (1yr), 56.25% (2yr), 46.88% (3yr), 37.50% (4yr), 28.13% (5yr). At end of term you can pay the residual and take ownership, refinance into a new lease, or trade in.

Running costs

Estimate of ~$2,720 + 4c per km: insurance $1,400, rego $720, servicing $350, tyres $250, plus electricity at home rates. Real quotes vary by postcode, driver age and EV model — adjust your expectations accordingly.

Lease admin fees

Most providers charge $300–700 per year plus an establishment fee. Not modelled here — add ~$2k–4k over 5 years to the novated total for a realistic figure.

Cash opportunity cost

Models the foregone return on cash that would otherwise sit in a mortgage offset, HISA or invested. Compounds annually. Set to 0% to compare nominal outlay only.

Used EV eligibility

For FBT exemption: vehicle must have been first held and used after 1 July 2022, and LCT must never have been payable. Most pre-2022 EVs and any EV originally above the LCT threshold are permanently disqualified.

Policy review

The Electric Car Discount is under federal review running 6 Feb 2026 to mid-2027. Existing leases are grandfathered, but new arrangements signed during or after the review may face changed rules.
